I am a postdoctoral research scientist at the National Centre for Atmospheric Science (NCAS), based at the University of Reading. My current project, [ReBASE](https://ed-hawkins.github.io/ReBASE/), concerns future changes in extreme weather and is led at Reading by Ed Hawkins. Prior to this, I completed my DPhil in Atmospheric Physics at Oxford under the supervision of Tim Woollings and Nick Dunstone, exploring multidecadal trends in initialised hindcasts. My DPhil work was a CASE project in collaboration with the Met Office. I first became interested in climate science during my physics degree at Imperial College London.
